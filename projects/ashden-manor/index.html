Questions couples ask

Things worth asking

Is 100 day guests enough?

It can sound small, but it makes for a far better day. You can talk to everyone you've invited, and it feels personal rather than processed. And 100 isn't the limit for the whole day: up to 150 people in total can join for the evening party.

Will it feel too formal?

It's an elegant house, but the day is yours to set the tone of. Come in your best, enjoy a long dinner, then take the party wherever you like. We aim for warm, not stuffy.

Is exclusive use worth it?

If you want privacy, unhurried photographs and a day that isn't fitted around anyone else, yes. No one else is on site, so there is nothing to work around and no one to share the gardens with.

How is this different from a hotel?

A hotel is open to everyone, all the time. Ashden Manor is handed over to you. It's your house for the day, with the gardens, the dining room and in-house catering all part of the same place.

Can we bring a pet?

Yes, we're pet-friendly. Tell us in your enquiry and we'll talk through it with you.

What will it cost?

It depends on your date and your guest numbers, so we'd rather give you a real figure than a vague range. Tell us roughly when and how many in the form below and we'll come back to you.
